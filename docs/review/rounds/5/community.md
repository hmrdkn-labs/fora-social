## Patterns

### P1 — major — Live notices cannot be dated from their standalone posters

- **Where:** all four sessions (`fora-ngobrol-karier`, `sigcn-gitops-argocd`, `sigcn-kubernetes-pemula`, `sigcn-observability`), `live/{instagram,x,linkedin}`. Poster files under each `examples/<session>/`: `ig-feed/live.png`, `ig-story/live.png`, `x/live.png`, `li-link/live.png`. The carousel/document topic slides do contain dates; the standalone images do not.
- **Quotes:** “LIVE SEKARANG”, “SEJAK 19.30 WIB” (career, GitOps, Kubernetes); “SEJAK 20.00 WIB” (Observability). The landscape sidebars say “LAGI JALAN” and show only “19.30–21.00 WIB” or “20.00–21.30 WIB”.
- **Why:** `jelas=3`. The joining route and immediate action are clear, but the rubric explicitly requires day and date on the poster. A saved, forwarded or resurfaced image cannot tell a member whether this is tonight's talk. A date on a different carousel slide does not repair the feed/story/landscape export.
- **Direction:** retain the immediate-entry hierarchy and put the session day/date beside the existing start time on each standalone live layout. Do not add another paragraph or repeat the invitation.

### I1 — major — The X speaker spotlight gives no reason to hear Sari or Rina

- **Where:** `sigcn-kubernetes-pemula/speakers/x`; `examples/sigcn-kubernetes-pemula/captions/speakers.md`, X 1/2–2/2, and `examples/sigcn-kubernetes-pemula/x/speakers.png`.
- **Quotes:** caption: “Bima membaca kubectl describe dari satu Pod (wadah tempat aplikasi jalan) yang gagal start.” Poster: “Sari (contoh)”, “Backend Developer”, “Rina (contoh)”, “Platform Engineer”.
- **Why:** `spesifik=3`. Bima has a concrete contribution, but the image reduces the other two people to job titles and the thread never recovers their contributions. This is the post whose job is to explain why these particular people, not just list a roster. Other platforms do supply Sari's first-deploy perspective and Rina's Pod experiment.
- **Direction:** use the existing thread to give the other people one concrete contribution each, or preserve short contribution lines on the landscape poster. Keep within the per-tweet limit; do not squeeze a complete bio into tiny text.

### I2 — major — Observability's X announcement is only logistics

- **Where:** `sigcn-observability/announce/x`; `examples/sigcn-observability/captions/announce.md`, X; `examples/sigcn-observability/x/announce.png`.
- **Quotes:** “Observability: Logs, Metrics, Traces”, “Kam, 14 Jan jam 20.00 WIB.”, “Masuk aja ke voice channel sig-cn-voice di Discord FORA.” Poster subtitle: “Dari mana mulai mencari, tanpa memasang semuanya sekaligus”.
- **Why:** `ngobrol=3`. The caption loses the recognisable slow-app/green-dashboard situation carried by IG and LinkedIn. The poster subtitle gives a direction but not the actual problem or a concrete thing to bring. A member can enter, but has little reason to reply or choose this session over another technical flyer.
- **Direction:** trade some caption boilerplate for the slow-app situation or one short diagnostic question; retain time and a usable entry route, with the poster carrying supporting details.

### I3 — minor — Career reminder uses two different practical details

- **Where:** `fora-ngobrol-karier/reminder/{instagram,linkedin}`; captions versus `ig-carousel/reminder-02.png` and `li-doc/reminder-02.png`.
- **Quotes:** IG caption: “Pakai headset kalau ada. Dari HP juga bisa.” LinkedIn caption: “Headset membantu, dan dari ponsel juga bisa.” Both topic slides: “Buka Discord sepuluh menit lebih awal buat cek suara.”
- **Why:** no score below 4. Both instructions are compatible and useful; this is not a factual contradiction. But the extra slide repeats the same sound-check advice as the three technical sessions rather than extending the career reminder's phone-friendly detail.
- **Direction:** align the practical-detail slide with the caption's headset/phone tip so the extra swipe adds the intended session-specific help.
