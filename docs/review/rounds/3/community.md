The prompt says 72 posts, but the current examples contain **7 post types × 4 sessions × 3 platforms = 84 posts**. All 84 are scored below; omitting `sig-intro` would silently exclude a requested post type. X thread parts count as one platform post. Captions and all attached formats are judged together except the rubric's poster-only clarity requirement.

## Patterns

### C1 · major · Public recap question assumes attendance

- **Where:** `sigcn-gitops-argocd/recap/instagram`, `sigcn-gitops-argocd/recap/x`, `sigcn-gitops-argocd/recap/linkedin`; caption (X part 3/3).
- **Quote:** “Dari alur tadi, bagian mana yang masih belum kebayang buatmu?”
- **Why:** `ngobrol` 3. “Alur tadi” addresses people who watched the walkthrough, whereas the social recap should also recruit replies from people who missed it. On X the preceding substantive tweet only explains separating manifest and application repositories; it does not actually show that flow. IG/LinkedIn supply useful notes, but the question still asks for feedback on an implied prior demonstration rather than on a concrete choice in those notes.
- **Direction:** Anchor one reply-sized question to a step actually present in the recap, such as manifest changes or sync, and make it answerable without having attended. Keep the useful technical notes and invitation.
