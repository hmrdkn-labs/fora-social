# Sample portraits

The five portraits here (`rina`, `bima`, `sari`, `dani`, `eko`) are **AI-generated placeholders of people who do not exist**. They were made with an image model for prototyping the speaker designs, one image per sample person, and stand in for the invented people in `examples/sessions/`.

Do not use them for a real session. Replace them with real photos that the person has agreed to have posted: square, at least 512 px, JPEG or PNG, face centred with some room above the head (the templates crop to a circle). A person without `photo:` gets the neutral silhouette from `brand/people/silhouette.svg`, as Dika does in the FORA sample.

Each file is 640 × 640, JPEG quality 82. The prompts are in the Report of `briefs/07-speaker-photos.md`.
