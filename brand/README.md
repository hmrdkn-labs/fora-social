# Brand

Logos, fonts, colours and tokens for FORA and its SIGs. Templates read from here; nothing else
in the repo defines colours or fonts.

## Typefaces

| Role | Face | Weights shipped | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sans, UI and body | [Montserrat](https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat) 9.000 | 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 | It is the face of the SIG CN lockup. |
| Serif, FORA mark and editorial accents | [Libertinus Serif](https://github.com/alerque/libertinus/releases/tag/v7.051) 7.051 | 400, 400 italic, 700 | Closest open match to the FORA mark. |

Both are SIL OFL 1.1; the licence sits next to the files in `fonts/<family>/OFL.txt`. Files are
WOFF2, converted from the upstream TTF/OTF without changes to the glyphs.

**Evidence** (images in `specimens/`):

- **SIG CN = Montserrat.** The cap height of "SIG" is 132 px in the PNG (font size about
  188.5 px). The "I" stem is 14 px, which is 74/1000 em: Montserrat Regular (Light is 51, Medium is
  100). "CN" and "CLOUD NATIVE" match Bold: the "N" is 641/1000 em wide (Bold 642) and the "C" is
  668 (Bold 668). See `specimens/montserrat-weights.png`.
- **FORA = a serif with a display-size TeX-style integral.** The PNG is most likely set in
  Cambria with Cambria Math's ∫ [INFERENCE: not provable from pixels]; neither is open.
  Candidates compared with the "FO" and "RA" glyphs at the PNG's cap height and positions
  (overlap score IoU, higher is better): **Libertinus Serif 0.72**, STIX Two Text 0.64, Caladea
  (Cambria clone) 0.61, Latin Modern Roman 0.58. Libertinus also reproduces the curled leg of
  the "R". See `specimens/fora-serif-candidates.png`.
- **The integral** is glyph `integral.v1` of Latin Modern Math 1.959. Its bounding box is 0.40
  wide/high, like the PNG's (160 × 401 px); STIX Two, Euler and Libertinus integrals are much
  heavier. Latin Modern Math is under the GUST Font License (free, LPPL-based). Only the
  outline is embedded in `tools/build_logos.py` and in the logo SVGs; the font is not shipped.
- The PNG letterforms are slightly bolder than Libertinus Regular; `specimens/logos-vs-png.png`
  shows the remaining difference in red.

## Logos

All in `logos/`, text converted to outlines (no font needed). Black variants for light
backgrounds, `-white` variants for dark ones (the "CN" stays blue on both).

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `fora-mark.svg` / `-white` | FORA alone: avatars, footers, general FORA posts. |
| `sigcn.svg` / `-white` | SIG CN wordmark (SIG / CN / CLOUD NATIVE) next to other elements. |
| `fora-x-sigcn.svg` / `-white` | The lockup: SIG CN session posts, covers. |
| `fora-logo.png`, `fora-x-sigcn.png`, `fora-x-sigcn-alt.png` | Originals, unchanged. Do not edit. |

- **Clear space:** keep a quarter of the ring diameter free around the mark; around the
  wordmark or lockup, the height of the "N" in "CN".
- **Minimum size:** mark 24 px (ring diameter), wordmark 96 px wide, lockup 200 px wide.
  Below that, use the mark alone.
- Do not recolour: black or white, plus the SIG blue in "CN".

Rebuild after changing geometry or fonts: `python3 brand/tools/build_logos.py`
(needs `fonttools` and `brotli`). Check against the PNGs:
`python3 brand/tools/compare_logos.py` (needs `pillow`, `numpy`, `resvg_py`).

## Colours

Tokens: `tokens.json` and `tokens.css` (same values, generated by `tools/build_tokens.py`).
Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ink` | `#0B0C10` | Text on light, dark backgrounds |
| `paper` | `#FFFFFF` | Light background, text on dark |
| `grey-50` … `grey-900` | see tokens | Panels, rules, secondary text |
| `sigcn` | `#0086FF` | SIG CN blue, sampled from the PNG lockup (the brief said about `#0A84FF`) |
| `sigcn-text` | `#0067C7` | Blue for text on light backgrounds |
| `sigcn-tint` | `#E5F3FF` | Light blue panel |
| `fora-accent` | `#F2A03D` | **Proposal, awaiting the person's approval** |

The FORA mark is black and white, so FORA has no official colour. `fora-accent` is a warm amber
chosen to sit next to SIG CN blue and to work on ink backgrounds; it is a proposal only.

| Text on background | Ratio | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink on paper | 19.55 | AA/AAA, any size |
| grey-700 on paper | 10.26 | AA/AAA |
| grey-500 on paper | 4.97 | AA (grey-500 on grey-50: 4.56, AA) |
| sigcn-text on paper | 5.58 | AA |
| sigcn-text on sigcn-tint | 4.94 | AA |
| paper on ink | 19.55 | AA/AAA |
| grey-300 on ink | 10.37 | AA/AAA |
| sigcn on ink | 5.45 | AA |
| fora-accent on ink | 9.19 | AA/AAA |
| ink on sigcn | 5.45 | AA: use ink text on blue fills |
| ink on fora-accent | 9.19 | AA/AAA |
| sigcn on paper | 3.59 | Large text (≥ 24 px bold / 32 px) and graphics only |
| paper on sigcn | 3.59 | Large text and graphics only: avoid, prefer ink on blue |

Spacing (4 px base), radii and the type scale for 1080-wide canvases are in the tokens. Smallest
text is 24 px (`label`); on 1600 and 1200 wide canvases scale by `width / 1080`.

## Do / don't

- Do use the white logo variants on dark and photo backgrounds, with a solid scrim behind.
- Do pair one SIG colour per post; the lockup already carries it.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or recolour the logos.
- Don't set body text in the serif; Libertinus is for the mark and short editorial accents.
- Don't put `fora-accent` or `sigcn` behind small white text.

## Adding a new SIG

1. Pick a colour and a dark-or-light `colorInk` that gives at least 4.5:1 on it; add the colour to
   `tools/build_tokens.py` and rerun it.
2. Build the wordmark the same way as `sigcn.svg`: a thin sans for the SIG, bold for the code and
   the name, converted to outlines. Copy `wordmark_paths` and `build` in `tools/build_logos.py`
   for the new letters, and write `logos/<id>.svg` plus a `-white` variant and a lockup with the
   FORA mark.
3. Add `sigs/<id>.yaml` with every field of `Sig` in `src/types.ts`: `id`, `short`, `code`, `name`,
   `color`, `colorInk`, `lockup` (repo-relative SVG), Indonesian `tagline` (one line) and
   `description` (2 to 4 sentences, say that sessions are casual voice-channel talks anyone can
   join), `hashtags` without `#`.
4. Look at the SVG next to its design in a browser before using it.
